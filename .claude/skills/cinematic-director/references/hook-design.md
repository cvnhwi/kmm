# Short-Form Hook Design

Use this reference when the request includes a hook, opening seconds, scroll-stopper, curiosity gap, or stronger opening for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, or an AI-generated narrative.

## Default behavior

- If given only a topic, infer a compact, emotionally clear, filmable story premise and state it briefly.
- Generate six meaningfully different hooks unless the user requests another number.
- When duration is unspecified, assume a vertical 20–30 second story with a 3-second hook.
- Lead with concrete action, an unusual image, a consequential reveal, or a sharply framed question. Skip greetings, logos, setup exposition, and vague claims.
- Establish stakes while withholding the decisive answer. The story must later deliver the promised payoff.
- For AI-generated video, prefer one dominant visual idea, readable silhouettes, simple continuity, and limited simultaneous action.

## Hook architecture

For each option provide:

1. A short name.
2. `0–1s`: exact first-frame image or action.
3. `1–3s`: escalation, interruption, or reversal.
4. `Text/voice`: one concise line only when it adds clarity or tension.
5. `Sound`: one purposeful cue, contrast, impact, or silence.
6. `Open loop`: the specific question created in the viewer's mind.
7. `Bridge`: how seconds 3–6 continue into the story without restarting or explaining away the opening.

Vary the mechanism across options. Useful patterns include consequence-first, interrupted action, visual contradiction, impossible object, mistaken identity, deadline, withheld recognition, before/after contrast, and partial reveal. Do not return superficial rewrites of one concept.

## Ranking

Score each option from 1–5 on:

- visual stopping power;
- curiosity/open-loop strength;
- emotional stakes;
- relevance to the real story and payoff;
- production feasibility and continuity.

Show the total out of 25. Select the strongest hook and justify it in one or two sentences. Add one concise `Avoid` note for the most likely failure: revealing the answer too early, overloading the frame, relying on text instead of action, weak bridging, or clickbait the story cannot repay.

Preserve the essential facts and ending of any supplied story. If a missing detail does not justify blocking, make a reasonable labeled assumption.

## Directing handoff

When cinematic execution is requested, preserve the selected hook's exact causal idea while translating it into shot size, angle, lens character, camera path, blocking, focus, time phases, peak frame, and stable end frame. The camera should clarify or intensify the open loop—not reveal its answer prematurely.

## Research basis

These principles synthesize TikTok's official [Creative Guidance](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/quicktok/online/tiktok_creative_accelerator/pc/en), [Video Advertising Guide](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/guides/video-advertising-guide), and [Top Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/inspiration/topads/pc/en): establish the hook immediately, communicate through native visual action, and bridge directly into the key message or story beat.
