## 1. Anti-blend rules (add to EVERY prompt with 2+ named characters)

1. **One line per image, full identity, with the Higgsfield @-tag (RULES J.9):** `@ImageN = <ROLE NAME> ONLY: <age/sex/build>, <hair/face>, <head item>, <top>, <bottom>, <shoes>, <prop>. Unique identifier: <the 1-2 things nobody else has>.`
2. **Add a [Cast Roll-Call] block:** "Exactly N people and the puppy, each appearing ONCE: ..." with a one-phrase tag per person, in the left-to-right order they stand in.
3. **Add a [No Blending] block** (verbatim):
   "Every character is a SEPARATE person who keeps ONLY the costume, colours, hair, face and props of their own reference image. Never mix or swap clothing, hats, helmets, hair or props between characters. Never merge two characters into one. Only the Soldier wears camouflage and a pith helmet; only the Police Officer wears a peaked cap; only the Engineer wears a hard hat; only the Cleaner wears a conical hat; only the bus driver wears a light-blue baseball cap; only Mom wears a white motorbike helmet and the yellow checked cape; only Dad wears the green bamboo-slat armour vest; only the Teacher wears an áo dài; only the Doctor wears a white coat; only the Firefighter wears the navy suit with yellow stripes and red helmet. Faces differ in age, shape and hair as described."
4. **Contrast pairs** (say them explicitly when both are in frame):
   - HIGHEST BLEND RISK: both wear olive short-sleeve shirts. Police Officer = young, PEAKED CAP, RED collar tabs + RED shoulder boards, gold buckle, very DARK trousers. Security Guard = about 50, BARE head with grey-streaked hair, NO red anywhere, green shield patch on the LEFT sleeve, trousers the SAME khaki as his shirt. Never stand them side by side in the blocking.
   - Soldier (camouflage, pith helmet) ≠ Police Officer (plain olive, peaked cap).
   - Bus driver (light-blue shirt and cap, moustache and goatee) ≠ Security Guard (khaki, bare head, clean-shaven).
   - Mai (bob haircut, sailor collar, pale-blue backpack) ≠ the schoolgirl friend (long double braids, denim backpack, tanned skin).
5. **Fewer references:** attach only the people who are actually visible. Above ~10 named people, put the less important ones in a far row, small in frame, and keep their lines short but still unique.
6. Avoid list adds: "characters blending together, swapped costumes, mixed uniforms, a cap or helmet on the wrong person, merged or hybrid characters, duplicated characters, generic extras replacing a named character".

## 3. BOSS arena = SUPER WIDE (applies to every BOSS-arena prompt, whatever angle plate is attached)

The BOSS arena is enormous, not a room. Even when a close angle plate (goc02, goc03, goc06, goc07, goc08, goc09...) is attached, the prompt must say:

"[Arena Scale] The BOSS arena is COLOSSAL: a vast open plaza the size of several football fields, enclosed by towering cliffs of stacked monitors as tall as skyscrapers that stretch away for hundreds of metres and fade into the cyan-teal fog; the far end is lost in haze; an open dark sky above instead of a low ceiling. The attached angle image shows only ONE corner/view of this huge space: keep its materials and design, but always show that the space continues far beyond it. People are tiny against the walls; in wide shots they occupy a small part of the frame, and even in medium shots the background shows the monitor towers receding far into deep haze, with strong aerial perspective and several layers of depth. Never a small room, a corridor, a low ceiling or close walls."

Avoid adds: "small room, narrow corridor, low ceiling, walls close behind the characters, cramped space, flat shallow background".

Camera help: prefer wide lenses (14-24 mm) for establishing shots; in medium shots keep a deep background (deep focus or a soft but deep haze) and frame so that a distant tower line is visible behind the characters.
